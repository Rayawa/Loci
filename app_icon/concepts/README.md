## 抽象版提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: alternative app icon concept for 时迹. Design a very refined, minimal, distinctive app icon communicating two separate concepts: daily life records and places. A square edge-to-edge deep ink-blue background with a gentle cool blue light from upper left, no border, no rounded tile mockup, no text. Center TWO separate sculptural geometric marks, clear negative space between them, not illustration objects: on the LEFT, three short warm ivory rounded horizontal strokes of different lengths descending like lines of a daily journal, aligned into a compact rhythm; on the RIGHT, one continuous apricot-orange rounded route stroke forming a simple open asymmetric loop, with one round endpoint indicating a place. Compose the two marks as an elegant balanced abstract emblem. Keep them distinct but part of one consistent visual language: same stroke weight, same rounded ends, restrained shallow extrusion and beautiful soft edge highlights, soft shadows lifting the marks above the background. Broad smooth shapes, generous negative space, calm premium identity, high contrast and recognizable at 48 pixels. No notebook, no calendar, no folded map, no clock, no map pin, no paper sheets, no checkboxes, no letters or numbers, no decorative particles or extra graphic elements. Precise optical balance, no toy-like glossy plastic. Actual square 1024x1024 artwork, both marks fully inside central 65% of canvas.

## 独立符号版提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: alternative actual app icon for 时迹, elegant restrained dimensional logo. A square 1024x1024 canvas filled edge to edge with very dark midnight-blue, softly illuminated from top left. No border or mockup frame or pre-rounded tile. Depict TWO separate highly simplified symbols, NOT one inside the other: a warm ivory circular day dial on the left-back (a clean solid circle with just two short recessed clock hands, no ticks or numbers) for recording daily life, and a smaller apricot-orange solid location marker at right-front for places. Their silhouettes must remain individually readable; the marker should sit mostly to the RIGHT of the dial, only a very small overlap for depth, the clock must NOT be inside the location marker. Deliberate asymmetric but balanced diagonal composition, generous surrounding empty space; entire emblem inside central 65% of canvas. Refined shallow 3D, solid satin ceramic surfaces, subtle bevels and side thickness, soft shadows and restrained warm highlights; matte and quiet, sophisticated visual identity, no heavy shiny plastic, no metallic gloss. Very few large details that read clearly at 48 pixels. No book, no paper, no folded map, no dense details, no text, letters, numbers, particles, sparkles or extra decorations.
