# 日历记录页＋地点标记 · 生成提示词

使用内置image_gen，三张真实项目图标仅作为风格和构图参考，生成新的时迹主题。参考来源见reference-design-notes.md。

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished app-icon design proposal for 时迹, a personal everyday-life-and-places journal. Three supplied images are STYLE/COMPOSITION REFERENCES ONLY: 1 Dashboard uses clearly recognizable functional symbols in one coherent emblem; 2 ElvaCos uses an elegant personalized focal form and soft dimensional highlights; 3 SmartShed uses a meaningful, coherent miniature subject scene with refined material depth. Do not copy their rocket, gears, letter E, cat ears, greenhouse, plant, or electronics. Design an original memorable symbol with the SAME clarity of app purpose and coherent art direction. Main subject: a handsome upright daily journal/calendar page on the left-back, and an independent coral/apricot-orange location marker on the right-front, composed together as ONE harmonious icon. The daily journal has TWO top binder rings, an ivory paper face, exactly THREE broad simple record rows, a slender icy-blue translucent backing and a gently curled lower paper corner; no words, numbers, letters or tiny checkbox detail. The location marker is a clear single classic teardrop silhouette with one round opening, modestly smaller than the calendar page, visually separate, placed BESIDE the page rather than embedded inside it, only slight overlap. Suggest a shared sense of everyday journeys with one very short gently curved blue path beneath these objects, no additional scene props. The two meanings must read immediately as daily records and places at small launcher size. Visual design: refined illustrative 2.5D icon, lightly sculpted paper and frosted glass, subtle slim beveled edges and clean soft contact shadows, controlled pastel-blue and warm coral colors, bright ivory paper. Richer custom craft than stock UI glyphs, but far fewer details than an illustration. Elegant optically balanced diagonal composition, quiet highlights, no bulky inflated toy/plastic look, no menu lines or abstract S-shaped logo. Background: uninterrupted very pale ice-blue to near-white softly lit gradient, full bleed, no navy background. Actual square 1024x1024 artwork, no mockup, no frame, no border, no pre-rounded app tile. Main composition fills central 72% with generous clean margins. No text, badges, numbers, stars, sparkles or decorative particles. It should feel like a carefully designed personal companion app, visually clear, fresh and mature.
